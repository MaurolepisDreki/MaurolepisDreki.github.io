The Art of Critical Thinking should be *first* nature (not second) to an Engineer.&nbsp; However, too often there are fundamental truths that many modern <q>engineers</q>[^1] are comming to outright reject:

# 1. Reality Exsits
Existance is *THE* Prime Axiom.&nbsp; There is no subject that can be talked about with opertating on the assumption that it exists in some shape or form.&nbsp; Even when we speak of non-existance we are speaking of the existential idea of the antithisis of existance &mdash; the cognative construct that is the absince of a thing.

So when we speak of Reality, we must assume that it too exists because if it did not exist then we could not speak of it.

And what is Reality?&nbsp; Reality is the state of Absolute Truth, the literal sum of all existance.&nbsp; Wherefore, then, is there any room to doubt that Reality exists?

*Dubito, ergo Cogito, ergo Sum.*  
I doubt, therefore I think, therefore I am.

Never before or since has the evidance of existance been put so simply:  
If I can think about something, then it must exist as an idea at the very least.&nbsp; And so if I can think about myself, even so much as to doubt my own existance, than I must exist, in fact, in order to be doing that thinking.&nbsp; Non-existant things do not exist, therefore they can take no actions and can have no effect on Reality because they have no presence in Reality.&nbsp; Therefore the fact that all real things must exist is Self-Evident and the Prime Axiom.


# 2. Reality is Objective
Reality is not a matter of perspective, it is the same for everyone.&nbsp; The Earth is round and could not be flat as a consequence of gravity &mdash; that is Reality, even despite that almost any given person's perspective would insist that the world is flat.&nbsp; Physiology is a function of Biochemestry which is primarily controled by DNA (&amp;c.) &mdash; that is Reality, and even the act of trying to alter or negate it requires that we acknowledge this as Fact and abide by the Laws of Nature which govern it.&nbsp;
