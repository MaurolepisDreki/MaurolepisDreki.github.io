I was intending to write a post titled <q class="title">10 things no engineer should need to be told</q>, and I was going to make the hightlight of the article about how engineering requires an unwavering dedication to Truth &mdash; truth being the absolute facts and laws which govern realty which cannot be altered or swayed by any number of perspectives or their preceived value.&nbsp; However, despite this remaining the truth, this truth being verifiable by every attempt of self-styled scientists and egineers to work their miracles in defiance of Truth, I find myself ill-qualified to write an article on the subject, and all the more so right now as I am attempting to rewrite parts of my psychology and repair my very soul with reconciled truths that would seem to be contradictory to some of the most fundimental truths of reality.

It would make more sense if I started at the beginning:

A few months ago I picked up a game that I had been putting off playing because they had yet to make available the race and gender of the character I wanted to create &mdash; the game is Final Fantasy XIV <q>Online,</q> and the character I did create there is a Female Hrothgar named Λиγᴇδ,[^1] and her person is intended to reflect the primary helpmeet that I have long sought [IRL]({% link codex/abbrv/IRL.md %}).&nbsp; So you can imagine my displeasure and distress when somebody on Discord[^2] leaked that the overabundance of Light she was absorbing from the LIghtwardens in the <q class="title">Shadowbringers</q> expansion was a threat to her health, which caused me to race through the rest of that storyline thinking that I was the only person in the world &mdash; in-game or out &mdash; who really wanted to save her and not just use her to stop the Eighth Umbral Calamity from anihilating the First and wreking irreperable havok back on the Source.

I am full-glad that I was wrong, that even among those who knew the risks of her tasks and declined to give me choice in matter, that throwing me under-the-bus and sacrificing her life for the greater good was the farthest thing from their intentions, and that they would have spent the rest of their greatly shortend lives to seek her salvation even knowing that the act of doing so would certainly cost them their own and that doing so would only achieve in sharing her end and make her into the unwilling cause of their demise.

But the whole ordeal set gears in motion that could not be stopped &mdash; *must* not be stopped, because, despite that by doing so some of my oldest and deepest wounds have been reopened, the reasserted injuries pertain to longstanding inibitors in my own life that I am now envious that she was was able to overcome.&nbsp; I envy the fact that she had such people in her life, fictitious although it may be, who would go so far to save her in her time of need as to sacifice their own lives and wellbeing for her sake just as she so often gave of herself unto them.&nbsp; I do not know where to find such people IRL, or if they even ever existed outside of their corrupted couterparts whose real-world deeds would bring about the same Great Calamity as Λиγᴇδ nearly gave her life to undo on the First &mdash; a Calamity of uncontested passivity and contentment not unlike what is commonly thought of as the Kingdom of Heaven right down to the very Earth itself being glassed over and thereby becoming incondusive to sustaining life in any form &mdash; what I do know is that I have long since needed such high-qualtiy people in my life to help counterballance the rest of them and make up for my rare boughts of weakness by proping me up when I lack the strength to stand and also acting as my shield rather than tossing me asside or letting me break when my own capacity to be theirs has reached its limmit or its end.

I have been burned and bled too many times, and by nearly every person I have ever known, and always by people who believe themselves to be rightious and justified in consuming the lives of others as a means of their own survival.&nbsp; Nobody should be able to fault me for being unable or apprehensive of giving anything more than I already have, and yet they always do.&nbsp; And yet I have so much more I could give, so much more that I want to give, and would too if it did not mean sacrificing what little life is now left to me to those same ungreatful people who denied me the capacity to replenish my life in the first place and do call me selfish for wanting fair compensation for my services.

Some would confuse the issue and claim that what I want is a one-sided contradition where I reap all the rewards of both justice and mercy and they get nothing &mdash; they misunderstand their situation: I do not seek, nor ever have sought, that kind of reversal of roles, unlike themselves who always do and most certainly would if our situations were reversed &mdash; what I seek is the exact enactment of merciless justice wherein the full price of my every action are repaid upon my own head, service for service, deed for equivent deed, and life for life even so much that my life might be secured, not by grace or charity, but by honor and virtue which demand that the debts incurred by being served are repaied by reciprocated service of our own.&nbsp; That is not benevolence, it is justice; that is not charity, it is equity; that is not grace, but it is virtue; and life on earth cannot be sustained by any other means.

And so the question arises, Where can I find the kind of people who are so... *good?*&nbsp; So noble and full of virtue, who give as good as they get, and then some?&nbsp; Who take nothing for granted and desire nothing more than to give the best they can offer so that they might, thereby, earn the right to trade it for the best that others can offer?  I would say that such people desire nothing more than to make the world a better place, and that would yet be the truth; but the best of people (those whom I seek) know all too well that the world cannot be made better by putting their service to the benift of those who would take as alms &mdash; they know that only way to make to make the world better is raise up others like themselves who could not live with themselves if they ever received something for nothing, and who would spend the rest of their lives trying to repay the kindness and generocity shown unto them by reciprocating it with greater kindness and generocity of their own.

It is a question that neither I nor anyone else whom I have ever known have ever been able to answer.
